# Reference photographs (Wikimedia Commons)

These photos are **visual references only**. None of them were downloaded into the repo or
used as textures. Every texture in `textures/` is generated procedurally by the scripts in
`blender/scripts/`. To check the photos, small thumbnails were downloaded to a temporary
scratch folder outside the repo.

Verification: on 2026-11-16, each File page URL below returned **HTTP 200**. Author and
licence come from the Commons API `extmetadata` fields (`Artist`, `LicenseShortName`).

Traits I took from these photos:
- The Oslo 2020-09-25 tree is a dense, very dark, conical crown. It is widest near the ground and its lower branches sweep down to the ground. This guided crown density and the low crown base of variant A.
- The Toten tree is a narrow, columnar spire with short upper branches. This is the narrow-crown end of the variant range.
- The two branch close-ups (Haugesund, Oslo 08-24) show the pinnate side shoots, needles 1-2 cm standing out all round the shoot, and paler new growth at the tips. These became the layout of the procedural spray texture.
- The bark photo shows a young, fairly smooth grey-brown trunk with small scales. Mature bark is more scaly and plated, so the texture is a compromise between the two.

Traits I took from these photos:
- Nyborgfurua and Tallodden show the long clear bole: grey-brown fissured bark low down, then orange-red bark above, and an irregular crown in the top third.
- Storfuruene and Mjøslandsfurua show flat-topped, umbrella-like old-tree crowns with heavy crooked limbs and foliage in dense dark clumps.
- Kristiansand shows a strongly crooked stem. Furu.JPG shows a pine on bare rock with surface roots. Neither form is modelled yet.
- **Gap:** the models are still much sparser and spikier than these photos. See ASSET_REPORT.md.

Traits I took from these photos (birch **models were NOT produced**; only the untested script `gen_birch.py` exists, and no birch textures are on disk):
- Trollheimen, Bjønnvatnet and Ljungdalen show mountain birch: several crooked white-grey stems from one base that lean outwards, with a broad irregular crown of small leaves. This is the basis of the `birch_mountain` parameters.
- Toten shows a tall single-stemmed downy birch with a narrow ovate crown and a dark fissured base. This is the basis of the `birch_lowland` parameters.

Traits I took from these photos:
- The Tangerud erratic is a rounded, mid-grey granite block with pale grey-green crustose lichen patches and a slightly buried, flattened base.
- Erdalen shows thick green moss mats on the upper surfaces. This drives the moss on upward-facing normals and at the ground contact.
- Senja shows vertical joint planes. Lindesnes shows a block perched on bedrock. Both informed the soft joint-plane facets.
